# Privacy Policy for Make a Wish

**Effective date:** October 10, 2026  
**Last updated:** October 10, 2026

Make a Wish Studio ("we", "us", or "our"), located in Ukraine, provides the **Make a Wish** mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, why it is used, when it may be shared, how long it is retained, and the choices available to you.

By using the App, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the App.

## 1. Information we collect

### 1.1 Account information

When you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication provides us with your Firebase user identifier (UID), display name, email address, and profile photo URL, where available. We use this information to authenticate you, keep your account secure, create your profile, and allow you to recover access across supported devices.

Your email address is not displayed to other users through the App. Your chosen nickname may be visible with public wishes.

### 1.2 Profile and gameplay information

We store information needed to operate your profile and App features, including:

- chosen nickname and profile photo URL;
- virtual coin balance;
- counts of wishes created and wishes liked;
- Mystery Coin timing and last-fortune state;
- Autumn Festival reward state;
- account creation and update timestamps;
- local preferences such as music on/off.

### 1.3 Wishes and user-generated content

You choose whether each wish is public or private.

- **Public wishes** may include your Firebase UID, nickname, wish text, like count, visibility/moderation status, and timestamps. They can be viewed by other signed-in users and reviewed by authorized moderators.
- **Private wishes** are associated with your account and are intended to be available only to you. They are not loaded into the moderation dashboard and are not submitted to the AI moderation feature. They are still stored and processed by our infrastructure providers as necessary to operate, secure, back up, and maintain the service or comply with law.

Do not include passwords, payment-card details, precise addresses, health records, government identifiers, or other highly sensitive information in a wish.

### 1.4 Likes, reports, and safety controls

We process records of wishes you like, authors you block, reports you submit, report reasons, moderation notices, and related timestamps. Reports may identify the reporting account, the reported public wish, and the reported author so that we can investigate abuse and enforce our rules.

### 1.5 Purchase information

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We may receive and store product identifiers, purchase/order identifiers, purchase tokens, purchase state, acknowledgement/consumption state, timestamps, and verification results to validate purchases, prevent duplicate credits and fraud, and deliver virtual coins.

We do **not** receive or store your complete payment-card number, bank credentials, or Google Play payment method details. Google handles payment information under its own privacy terms.

### 1.6 Technical, integrity, and security information

Firebase App Check, Google Play Integrity, Firebase, Google Play, and Cloudflare may process technical information such as App/package identity, integrity verdicts, device or app-instance signals, IP address, request metadata, authentication tokens, and security logs. We use these signals to prevent automated abuse, unauthorized clients, fraud, and attacks.

### 1.7 Information we do not intentionally collect

The App does not intentionally request precise GPS location, contacts, SMS messages, call logs, microphone recordings, camera images, or advertising identifiers. We do not use third-party behavioral advertising or sell personal information.

## 2. How we use information

We use information to:

- authenticate users and maintain accounts;
- provide wishes, profiles, likes, reports, virtual coins, rewards, and other App features;
- display public wishes and their authors' nicknames;
- store private wishes for the account owner;
- verify and fulfill Google Play purchases;
- detect prohibited content, fraud, abuse, compromised clients, and security incidents;
- review reports, warn users, hide or restore public content, and enforce our Terms of Use;
- troubleshoot, maintain, and improve reliability;
- comply with legal obligations and protect users, the public, and our rights.

Depending on your country, we rely on performance of our contract with you, our legitimate interests in operating and securing the App, your consent where required, and compliance with legal obligations. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it, but this does not affect earlier lawful processing.

## 3. Public content and moderation

Public wishes and nicknames are user-generated content. They may be visible to other users and may be copied or captured by recipients outside our control.

Public wishes and nicknames may be checked by client-side filters and sent to our Cloudflare Worker and Cloudflare Workers AI moderation service for automated safety classification. Automated systems can make mistakes. Authorized moderators may review public content and reports and may hide, restore, or remove content or take account action. Private wishes are excluded from this AI/admin moderation workflow.

## 4. When information is shared

We share information only as needed for the purposes described above:

- **Google and Firebase:** Google Sign-In/Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase App Check, Play Integrity, and Google Play Billing/purchase verification.
- **Cloudflare:** Workers infrastructure for secure backend operations and purchase verification, and Workers AI for moderation of public wishes and nicknames.
- **Other users:** your nickname, public wish text, public like count, and related public content.
- **Authorities or safety recipients:** where reasonably necessary to comply with law, legal process, protect rights or safety, investigate fraud or abuse, or respond to an emergency.
- **Business transfer:** if the App or its assets are reorganized, sold, or transferred, subject to appropriate safeguards and applicable law.

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Service providers may process information in countries outside Ukraine or your country. We use providers' contractual, organizational, and technical safeguards and take reasonable steps required by applicable law for international transfers.

## 5. Retention

We keep information only as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App, maintain security, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations.

- Account/profile data and wishes are generally retained while your account is active or until deleted.
- Public content may be hidden before deletion when required for moderation or investigation.
- Reports, purchase-verification records, fraud-prevention records, and security logs may be retained after account deletion where reasonably necessary for legal compliance, accounting, dispute resolution, abuse prevention, or enforcement.
- Google Play may retain transaction records independently under Google's policies and legal obligations.
- Residual copies may remain temporarily in encrypted backups or provider systems until normal deletion cycles complete.

We may anonymize information so it can no longer identify you; anonymized information may be retained longer.

## 6. Account and data deletion

You can delete your account inside the App:

1. Open **Profile**.
2. Open **Settings**.
3. Tap **Delete account**.
4. Confirm and complete Google re-authentication if requested.

This process removes the Firebase Authentication account and App data associated with it, including profile data, authored public and private wishes, likes, blocks, reports, purchase-delivery records, and notices, subject to the limited retention described above.

Uninstalling the App does not delete your account. If you cannot access the App, email **makeawishgame@gmail.com** from the Google email associated with the account, use the subject **Make a Wish account deletion**, and include your nickname. Never send us your password, full card number, or Google password. We may request reasonable verification before deletion. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days unless a longer period is required or permitted by law.

## 7. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or withdrawal of consent, and to complain to a competent data-protection authority.

You can edit your nickname in the App, remove likes, block authors, delete your account, and choose whether a new wish is public or private. To exercise other rights, contact **makeawishgame@gmail.com**. We may verify your identity and may decline or limit a request where permitted by law, including to protect other users' rights, prevent fraud, or comply with legal obligations.

## 8. Children's privacy

The App currently carries a **12+** content rating on Google Play. A store content rating does not determine whether a child can legally consent to data processing in a particular country.

The App is intended for a general audience and is not primarily directed to children. A person who cannot legally consent to account creation or data processing under applicable local law may use the App only with authorization and supervision of a parent or legal guardian. If you believe a child provided personal information without required authorization, contact us and we will investigate and take appropriate action, including deletion where required.

No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee that unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disruption will never occur.
