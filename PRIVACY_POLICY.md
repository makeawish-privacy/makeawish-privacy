# Privacy Policy for Make a Wish

**Last updated: December 23, 2024**

**Effective date: December 23, 2024**

---

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to Make a Wish ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ("App").

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information
When you use Make a Wish, we collect:
- **Email address** — obtained through Google Sign-In
- **Display name** — obtained through your Google account profile
- **Profile photo URL** — obtained through your Google account
- **User ID** — unique identifier for your account

### 2.2 User-Generated Content
- **Wishes** — text content you create within the app (both private and public)
- **Comments** — comments you leave on other users' public wishes
- **Nickname** — custom nickname you set in the app
- **Likes** — records of wishes you have liked

### 2.3 Purchase Information
- We facilitate in-app purchases through Google Play
- **We do NOT collect or store** your payment card details, billing address, or financial information
- All payment processing is handled securely by Google Play

### 2.4 Automatically Collected Information
- **Device information** — device type, operating system version
- **App usage data** — features used, interactions within the app
- **Timestamps** — when wishes and comments are created

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Provide, operate, and maintain the App
- Allow you to create, save, and share wishes
- Display your nickname and avatar on public wishes
- Process in-app purchases for virtual coins
- Save your progress, preferences, and coin balance
- Respond to your inquiries and support requests
- Improve and personalize the App experience

---

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), we process your personal data based on:
- **Consent** — you provide consent when signing in with Google
- **Contract** — processing is necessary to provide the App's services
- **Legitimate interests** — to improve our App and prevent fraud

---

## 5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Firebase (Google Cloud Platform):
- **Firebase Authentication** — for secure sign-in
- **Firebase Firestore** — for storing wishes, comments, and user data

**Security measures:**
- All data transmission is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS protocols
- Data is stored on secure Google Cloud servers
- Access to user data is restricted and protected

---

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as:
- Your account is active
- Necessary to provide our services
- Required by applicable law

You may request deletion of your data at any time (see Section 8).

---

## 7. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do **NOT sell** your personal information.

We share data only with:
| Third Party | Purpose | Data Shared |
|-------------|---------|-------------|
| Google Firebase | Data storage & authentication | Account info, wishes, preferences |
| Google Play | In-app purchases | Purchase transactions (handled by Google) |

## 8. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access** — request a copy of your personal data
- **Rectification** — correct inaccurate personal data
- **Deletion** — request deletion of your account and all associated data
- **Restriction** — limit how we process your data
- **Portability** — receive your data in a portable format
- **Withdraw consent** — stop using the App at any time

**To exercise these rights**, contact us at the email provided below.

**Data deletion requests** will be processed within 30 days.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Make a Wish is **NOT intended for children under 13 years of age**.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

## 10. In-App Purchases

Make a Wish offers optional in-app purchases for virtual coins.
- All purchases are processed through **Google Play**
- We do not have access to your payment details
- Purchases are subject to Google Play's Terms of Service
- Virtual coins have no real-world monetary value and cannot be exchanged for cash
